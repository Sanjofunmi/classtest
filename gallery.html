Featured Focus: The Pollinator & Orchid Pavilion

The Pollinator Pavilion offers an immersive walkthrough environment where hundreds of native and tropical butterflies roam freely among indigenous nectar blossoms. High atmospheric humidity and balanced sunlight foster optimal pollination dynamics.

Documenting Sanctuary Biodiversity

Our resident field photographers and scientific staff regularly catalogue changing seasons across the reserve. From the delicate unfurling of spring fiddlehead ferns to autumn canopy transformations, photographic documentation helps us monitor micro-habitat stability over multiple decades.

Visitors are invited to bring non-commercial cameras and sketchpads during regular opening hours. Early morning guided photography walks are held on the first Saturday of each month, offering access before public hours when lighting is softest and wildlife activity peaks.
